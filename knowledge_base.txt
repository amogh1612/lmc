## Eleanor Whitfield
Eleanor Whitfield (1842–1919) was the founder of Southview's first public library
and a leading advocate for women's education in the county. She is buried in the
Whitfield family plot near the east gate. Her tombstone is notable for its carved
open-book motif, a tribute to her library work.

## Colonel James T. Hargrove
Colonel James T. Hargrove (1820–1888) served in the local militia and later became
the town's second mayor. He is credited with establishing the cemetery itself in
1861 on land donated from his family farm. His grave, marked by a granite obelisk,
sits at the highest point of the cemetery grounds.

## Dr. Maria Consuelo Reyes
Dr. Maria Consuelo Reyes (1901–1976) was the town's first female physician and
ran a free clinic during the Great Depression. She is remembered for treating
patients regardless of their ability to pay. Her grave marker includes a small
carved caduceus symbol.

## Samuel P. Ostrander
Samuel P. Ostrander (1858–1932) was a railroad engineer who helped bring the
regional rail line through town in the 1880s, spurring local economic growth.
He is buried alongside his wife, Clara Ostrander, in the northern section of
the cemetery.
