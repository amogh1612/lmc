## Person 1: Rev. Martin Luther King Sr. ("Daddy King")

### Takeaway
Born Michael King in Stockbridge, GA, he pastored Ebenezer Baptist Church for 44 years (from 1931), was an Atlanta NAACP and civic-political leader who pushed Black voter registration and teacher-salary equalization in the 1930s-40s, died in Atlanta on Nov 11, 1984, and is buried at South-View beside his wife Alberta under a combined marker. His birth year (1897 vs 1899) and the year of his City Hall voting march (1936 vs 1939) conflict across sources.

### Cited Findings

**Identity and vital facts**
- Born Michael King; later changed his name (and his eldest son's) to Martin Luther King — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr); [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- Name change dated to 1934 per Wikipedia, citing the Papers of Martin Luther King Jr., Vol. I (1992), pp. 30-31 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Sr.) (lead only; underlying source is the King Papers)
- Nickname "Daddy King" — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795); also title of his autobiography (see below)
- **CONFLICT – birth year:** Stanford says "born Michael King on 19 December 1897, in Stockbridge, Georgia" — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr); Find a Grave and Wikipedia give 19 Dec 1899, Stockbridge, Henry County, GA — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Sr.); the Digital Library of Georgia/CRDL authority record also uses "1899-1984" — [CRDL](https://crdl.usg.edu/people/king_martin_luther_sr_1899_1984). Age "84" at death (NYT headline "Rev. King dies at 84", per Wikipedia citation) is consistent with 1899.
- Died November 11, 1984, in Atlanta — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr); [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- **CONFLICT – cause of death:** Heart attack at Crawford W. Long Hospital, aged 84 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Sr.) (citing NYT obituary by Peter Kerr, Nov 12, 1984: https://www.nytimes.com/1984/11/12/obituaries/rev-king-dies-at-84-with-son-a-leader-in-rights-movement.html — NYT page could not be fetched to verify); a cemetery blog says he "died after a stroke" — [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/). Stanford does not state a cause.

**Burial**
- Buried at South-View Cemetery, Atlanta; he and Alberta King lie beneath a combined marker — [AJC-HistoricSites](https://www.ajc.com/lifestyles/holiday/martin-luther-king-atlanta-other-historic-sites/HNHODP0HjozTYTzOf4V2sK/)
- Find a Grave plot: South-View Cemetery, "Next to First Road paralleling Jonesboro Road" — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- Tomb of King Sr. and Alberta is near the front gates of South-View; he was buried beside her — [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/)
- South-View address: 1990 Jonesboro Road, Atlanta; founded 1886 by formerly enslaved people and Black businessmen — [AJC-HistoricSites](https://www.ajc.com/lifestyles/holiday/martin-luther-king-atlanta-other-historic-sites/HNHODP0HjozTYTzOf4V2sK/)

**Family**
- Parents: James and Delia King; he was their eldest son — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr). Find a Grave gives James Albert "Jim" King Sr. (1869–1936) and Delia "Linsey" King (1875–1924) — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- Married Alberta Christine Williams on Thanksgiving Day 1926 at Ebenezer — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Children: Willie Christine (b. 1927), Michael Luther Jr. [Martin Luther King Jr.] (b. 1929), Alfred Daniel Williams (b. 1930) — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Daughter Christine King Farris lived 1927–2023; sibling Rev. Joel Lawrence King Sr. (1915–1996) — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- Father-in-law Rev. A. D. Williams preceded him as Ebenezer pastor — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)

**Education**
- Stockbridge Colored School (three to five months a year); Bryant Preparatory School; admitted to Morehouse College 1926 — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- **CONFLICT – Morehouse dates:** Stanford implies bachelor's in theology c. 1930 (A.D. born "a month after" he received it) — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr); Wikipedia says began theology study at Morehouse 1925 and earned a Bachelor of Theology in 1931 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Sr.)
- Honorary Doctor of Humane Letters, Dillard University, 1975 — [Dillard honorees (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20230621071651/https://www.dillard.edu/commencement/honorees.php) (via Wikipedia lead)

**Career timeline**
- 1918: Left Stockbridge for Atlanta, joining his sister Woodie — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Early 1920s: Pastored several churches in College Park while studying — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- 1931: Succeeded A. D. Williams as pastor of Ebenezer Baptist Church; served Ebenezer 44 years total — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- 1934: Attended the Baptist World Alliance in Berlin — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Served on boards of Atlanta University, Morehouse College, and the National Baptist Convention — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- 1960: Helped mobilize Black support for John F. Kennedy — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795)
- 1964: Traveled to Oslo for MLK Jr.'s Nobel Peace Prize ceremony — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Fall 1975: Reportedly first African American to address a joint session of the Alabama legislature — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795) (single, low-authority source; treat as unverified)
- 1976 and 1980: Delivered invocations at the Democratic National Convention; strong supporter of Jimmy Carter — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)

**Civil rights activity**
- Local leader in the NAACP and the Atlanta Civic and Political League — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- 1935: With other ministers of the Atlanta Baptist Ministers Union, called for Black voter registration — [Stanford chronology](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/atlanta-baptist-ministers-union-calls-black-voter-registration)
- March 6, 1936: Spoke at an NAACP mass meeting at Wheat Street Baptist Church — [Stanford chronology](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-sr-speaks-naacp-mass-meeting-atlanta)
- **CONFLICT – City Hall voting march year:** Find a Grave says in 1936 he "led the first black voting rights march in the history of Atlanta" — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9795); 11Alive says in 1936 he led a march of several hundred on City Hall — [11Alive](https://11alive.com/article/news/local/black-history/rev-martin-luther-king-sr-advocate-for-social-change/85-68774415) (not fetched directly; per search snippet); Stanford's encyclopedia says that in **1939** he proposed a voter registration drive to begin with a march to City Hall — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- Personally defied segregation, e.g., riding the "whites only" City Hall elevator to reach the voter registrar — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- 1940: Chairman of the Committee on the Equalization of Teachers' Salaries; with NAACP legal help the movement "resulted in significant gains for black teachers" — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- During the Montgomery bus boycott, unsuccessfully urged his son not to return to Montgomery — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)

**Published works / legacy**
- Autobiography *Daddy King: An Autobiography* (1980), William Morrow, co-written with Riley (Stanford gives surname only; full co-author name not verified in fetched sources) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Sr.) (publisher/year); Stanford footnotes cite it as co-written with Riley, 1980 — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)

**Notable visitor facts**
1. He was born Michael King and changed his and his son's name to Martin Luther King — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
2. Pastor of Ebenezer Baptist Church for 44 years, starting 1931 — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
3. Gave the invocation at the 1976 and 1980 Democratic National Conventions — [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
4. Outlived his wife and both sons; buried beside Alberta under a combined marker at South-View — [AJC-HistoricSites](https://www.ajc.com/lifestyles/holiday/martin-luther-king-atlanta-other-historic-sites/HNHODP0HjozTYTzOf4V2sK/)
5. MLK Jr. was also first buried at South-View after April 1968, then moved about two years later to the King Center tomb on Auburn Avenue — [AJC-HistoricSites](https://www.ajc.com/lifestyles/holiday/martin-luther-king-atlanta-other-historic-sites/HNHODP0HjozTYTzOf4V2sK/)

### Inferences
- The 1936 vs 1939 discrepancy may reflect two distinct events (a 1936 march vs a 1939 proposed registration drive/march); Stanford is the most authoritative source and does not mention a 1936 march, so the chatbot should present the 1936 march as "widely reported" and note Stanford's 1939 account.
- 1899 is the more commonly used birth year (Find a Grave, Wikipedia, CRDL, age 84 at death); Stanford's 1897 should be flagged.

## Person 2: Alberta Williams King ("Mother King")

### Takeaway
Daughter of Ebenezer pastor A. D. Williams, Alberta Williams King was Ebenezer's organist from 1932 to 1972 and founded its choir. She was shot and killed by Marcus Wayne Chenault while playing the organ during Sunday service on June 30, 1974, and is buried with her husband at South-View. Her birth year (1903 vs 1904) and age at death (70 vs 69) conflict across sources.

### Cited Findings

**Identity and vital facts**
- Full maiden name Alberta Christine Williams — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796); [Stanford-KingSr](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-martin-luther-sr)
- **CONFLICT – birth year/age:** Stanford: born September 13, 1903, in Atlanta; died "later that day, at age 70" — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams); Find a Grave: born 13 Sep 1904, Atlanta; died age 69 — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796). Wikipedia/other outlets also say 69 (per search results) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Williams_King)
- Died June 30, 1974, Atlanta — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams); [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)
- Note: the "Mother King" nickname was not confirmed in any fetched source (see Gaps).

**Burial**
- Buried at South-View Cemetery, Atlanta, with her husband beneath a combined marker — [AJC-HistoricSites](https://www.ajc.com/lifestyles/holiday/martin-luther-king-atlanta-other-historic-sites/HNHODP0HjozTYTzOf4V2sK/)
- Plot "Next to First Road paralleling Jonesboro Road"; inscription "...Her Life Shall Be Filled With Music..." — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)
- Tomb near South-View's front gates — [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/)
- Her parents, Rev. A. D. Williams and Jennie Parks Williams, are also buried at South-View, with two children who died in infancy — [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/)

**Family**
- Parents: Jennie Celeste Williams and Rev. Adam Daniel (A. D.) Williams, pastor of Ebenezer; she was their only surviving child — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams). Find a Grave dates: A. D. Williams 1863–1931; Jennie Celeste Parks Williams 1875–1941; infant siblings Fannie Louise (1906–1907) and Parks (1908) — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)
- Married Michael King (later Martin Luther King Sr.) on Thanksgiving Day 1926 — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- Children: Willie Christine, Martin Luther King Jr., Alfred Daniel — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)

**Education**
- Spelman Seminary (high school); Hampton Normal and Industrial Institute (teaching certificate); BA, Morris Brown College, 1938 — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- Hampton teaching certificate dated March 1924 — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)

**Career timeline**
- Pre-1926: Taught briefly; stopped because the local school board barred married women from teaching — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- 1932–1972: Organist at Ebenezer; founded the Ebenezer choir — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- 1950–1962: Organist for the Women's Auxiliary of the National Baptist Convention — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- 1950–1962: Organizer and president of the Ebenezer Woman's Council — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)
- Active in the YWCA, NAACP, and Women's International League for Peace and Freedom — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)

**Assassination (June 30, 1974)**
- Shot during Sunday services at Ebenezer while playing the organ, by Marcus Chenault of Ohio, who claimed "All Christians are my enemies"; she died later that day — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
- She was playing "The Lord's Prayer"; gunman Marcus Wayne Chenault Jr. also hit deacon Edward Boykin (killed) and congregant Jimmie Mitchell (survived) — [Atlanta Magazine](https://www.atlantamagazine.com/history/the-murder-of-alberta-king/) and [WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/king-family-remembers-alberta-williams-king-50-years-after-ebenezer-baptist-church-deadly-shooting/C5IS77OIMJEBHCPCHIR5QEX3N4) (per search snippets; pages returned 403/not fetched)
- Chenault's reported original target was MLK Sr.; he claimed insanity, was convicted and sentenced to death — [NNPA](https://nnpa.org/?p=94045) (per search snippet)
- **CONFLICT – Chenault's age:** 21 per [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams); 23 per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Williams_King) (per search snippet)

**Notable visitor facts**
1. Killed at the organ of Ebenezer Baptist Church, June 30, 1974, six years after her son's assassination — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
2. Played organ at Ebenezer for 40 years (1932–1972) and founded its choir — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)
3. Her gravestone reads "...Her Life Shall Be Filled With Music..." — [Find a Grave](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/9796)
4. Her father A. D. Williams was Ebenezer's pastor before her husband; her parents are also at South-View — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams); [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/)
5. Earned a BA from Morris Brown College in 1938, as a married mother of three — [Stanford-Alberta](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alberta-williams)

### Inferences
- Stanford's 1903 birth year is consistent with its "age 70"; Find a Grave's 1904 with "69". Present both and flag.

## Person 3: Rev. Alfred Daniel Williams King ("A.D. King")

### Takeaway
MLK Jr.'s younger brother (b. July 30, 1930, Atlanta), a Baptist pastor in Newnan, Birmingham-Ensley and Louisville, whose Birmingham home was bombed May 11, 1963, and who won a Louisville open-housing ordinance in 1968. He was found drowned in his home swimming pool in Atlanta on July 21, 1969 (ruled accidental; family disputes this) and is buried at South-View.

### Cited Findings

**Identity and vital facts**
- Full name Alfred Daniel Williams King; known as "A. D." to family and friends — [Stanford-ADKing](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alfred-daniel-williams)
- Born July 30, 1930, Atlanta — [Stanford-ADKing](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alfred-daniel-williams); [AJC-ForgottenKing](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/the-forgotten-king/H9AOYuODaj2VyFgHYdtbBO/)
- Died July 21, 1969, age 38, by drowning — [Stanford-ADKing](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alfred-daniel-williams)
- Found at the bottom of the family swimming pool at his Atlanta home the morning after a July 20, 1969 phone call; ruled accidental; son Derek found him; wife Naomi was vacationing in Jamaica — [AJC-ForgottenKing](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/the-forgotten-king/H9AOYuODaj2VyFgHYdtbBO/)
- **DISPUTE – cause:** Naomi King: "Absolutely, he was murdered"; daughter Alveda: "Daddy was killed and put in the pool"; Andrew Young believed it was a heart attack and "There was never any evidence of foul play" — [AJC-ForgottenKing](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/the-forgotten-king/H9AOYuODaj2VyFgHYdtbBO/)
- **CONFLICT – age at death:** 38 per [Stanford-ADKing](https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/king-alfred-daniel-williams) and [AJC-ForgottenKing](https://www.ajc.com/news/local/the-forgotten-king/H9AOYuODaj2VyFgHYdtbBO/) (correct by dates); 39 per [Blog-SouthView](https://adventuresincemeteryhopping.com/2015/10/30/free-at-last-visiting-atlantas-south-view-cemetery-part-iii/)
